# Contract with Keystone Claims Services for Claimllm

**Contract Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Contract Duration:** 18 months

---

## Terms

1. **Parties Involved**: This contract is entered into between Insurellm (the "Provider") and Keystone Claims Services (the "Customer"), an independent third-party administrator handling property and auto claims for regional insurers.

2. **License Grant**: Insurellm grants Keystone Claims Services a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Claimllm under the Advanced Tier at $9,500/month, covering up to 25,000 claims annually.

3. **Overage Fees**: Claims processed beyond 25,000 in a contract year will be billed at $0.45 per claim.

4. **Payment Terms**: Monthly payments are due on the 10th of each month. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

5. **Confidentiality**: Both parties shall keep each other's proprietary information confidential during the contract and for three years after termination.

## Renewal

1. **Automatic Renewal**: This contract shall automatically renew for additional 12-month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 45 days prior to expiration.

2. **Volume Review**: If Keystone's annual claim volume exceeds 25,000 for two consecutive quarters, both parties will negotiate a move to the Enterprise Tier.

## Features

1. **Included Features**: Under the Advanced Tier, Keystone Claims Services will have access to:
   - Intelligent FNOL Processing
   - Automated Triage and Routing
   - Computer Vision Damage Assessment
   - Predictive Fraud Detection
   - Smart Document Processing
   - Dynamic Reserve Setting
   - Vendor Management Platform
   - Claimant Communication Hub
   - Analytics and Reporting

2. **Excluded Features**: Payment Automation is not included; Keystone will continue to use its existing payment system, integrated via Claimllm's standard export.

3. **White-Label Portal**: Not included in this tier. Claimant-facing communications will carry Keystone branding through configurable templates.

## Support

1. **Customer Support**: Insurellm will provide 24/7 support for critical incidents and business-hours support for general inquiries.

2. **Training**: Insurellm will train up to 60 Keystone adjusters across three remote sessions in the first month.

3. **Performance Reporting**: Insurellm will deliver a monthly report covering average cycle time, straight-through processing rate, and fraud referral accuracy.
