# Contract with Cascade Health Partners for Healthllm

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025  
**Contract Duration:** 12 months

---

## Terms

1. **Parties to the Agreement**: This contract is entered into between Insurellm, hereafter referred to as "Provider," and Cascade Health Partners, a regional health plan serving members in Oregon and Washington, hereafter referred to as "Client."

2. **Scope of Services**: Provider grants the Client access to the Healthllm platform under the Essential Tier for administration of the Client's individual and small-group health plans.

3. **Fees**: The Client agrees to pay $8,000/month, invoiced monthly and payable within 30 days of invoice.

4. **HIPAA Compliance**: Both parties will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) prior to the transfer of any protected health information. Provider will maintain HIPAA-compliant safeguards for all member data.

5. **Termination**: Either party may terminate this agreement with 90 days' written notice. The Client may terminate immediately in the event of a material data breach caused by Provider.

## Renewal

1. **Automatic Renewal**: This contract will automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

2. **Tier Upgrade**: The Client may upgrade to the Professional Tier ($15,000/month) at any time; the new rate applies from the following billing cycle.

## Features

1. **Included Features**: Under the Essential Tier, the Client will have access to:
   - Intelligent Plan Design
   - Real-Time Eligibility Verification
   - AI-Driven Claims Adjudication
   - Member Engagement Platform
   - Regulatory Compliance Engine

2. **Excluded Features**: Predictive Healthcare Analytics, Provider Network Management, and Medication Management are not included in the Essential Tier.

3. **Integrations**: Standard integrations with the Client's clearinghouse and pharmacy benefit manager are included.

## Support

1. **Customer Support**: Provider will offer email and phone support during business hours (7am-6pm PT), with emergency on-call support for system outages.

2. **Implementation**: Provider will complete implementation and member data migration within 90 days of the effective date.

3. **Service Level Agreement**: Provider guarantees 99.5% platform uptime per calendar month. If uptime falls below this level, the Client will receive a 10% credit on that month's fee.
