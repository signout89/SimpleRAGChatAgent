# Contract with Ironclad Mutual Insurance for Bizllm

**Contract Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Contract Duration:** 24 months

---

## Terms

1. **Parties Involved**: This contract is entered into between Insurellm (the "Provider") and Ironclad Mutual Insurance (the "Customer"), a multi-line commercial carrier headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

2. **License Grant**: Insurellm grants Ironclad Mutual Insurance a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Bizllm platform under the Professional Tier at $12,000/month.

3. **Payment Terms**: Ironclad Mutual Insurance agrees to make monthly payments of $12,000, due on the 1st of each month. A one-time implementation fee of $18,000 is payable within 30 days of the contract date.

4. **Confidentiality**: Both parties shall maintain confidentiality regarding each other's proprietary information during the contract and for three years following its termination.

5. **Data Ownership**: All policyholder and underwriting data remains the property of Ironclad Mutual Insurance. Insurellm will return or securely delete all Customer data within 60 days of contract termination.

## Renewal

1. **Automatic Renewal**: This contract shall automatically renew for additional one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to expiration.

2. **Pricing Review**: Renewal pricing will be capped at a 5% increase over the prior term's monthly fee.

## Features

1. **Included Features**: Under the Professional Tier, Ironclad Mutual Insurance will have access to:
   - Multi-Line Underwriting Engine
   - Business Intelligence Integration
   - Cyber Risk Assessment
   - Workers' Compensation Optimization
   - Commercial Property Evaluation
   - Portfolio Management Dashboard
   - Agent and Broker Portal

2. **Optional Add-On**: Professional Liability Specialization may be added for $2,000/month with 30 days' written notice.

3. **Integrations**: Insurellm will integrate Bizllm with Ironclad's existing Guidewire PolicyCenter instance during implementation.

## Support

1. **Customer Support**: Insurellm will provide business-hours support (8am-8pm ET) by phone and email, with a 4-hour response target for critical issues.

2. **Training**: Insurellm will deliver onboarding training for up to 40 underwriters and 25 broker partners within the first 45 days.

3. **Quarterly Business Reviews**: Insurellm will review loss ratio trends, platform usage, and roadmap priorities with Ironclad's leadership each quarter.
